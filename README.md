# Debt thresholds and monetary sovereignty

**Does the "90% debt threshold" depend on whether a country borrows in a currency it issues?**

This project replicates the debt–growth tables of Reinhart & Rogoff (2010), *Growth in a Time of Debt*,
following the corrected replication by Herndon, Ash & Pollin (2014), and then extends it with one
question the original debate left out: the **monetary regime** of the borrowing country. Growth by
debt/GDP bucket is compared separately for issuers of a floating own currency and for countries under
a peg or a monetary union.

Part of the empirical groundwork for my bachelor's thesis (University of Economics in Katowice):
*"Sześćdziesiąt procent czego? Adekwatność miar długu publicznego w świetle nowoczesnej teorii
monetarnej na przykładzie Polski"* — on the adequacy of public debt measures in the light of
Modern Monetary Theory.

> Status: work in progress.

